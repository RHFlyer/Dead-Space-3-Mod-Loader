Third-party software in this release
====================================

1. MinHook 1.3.4 (https://github.com/TsudaKageyu/minhook)
   Compiled into dinput8.dll. BSD 2-clause licence; its full notice follows.

2. Raw Input Mouse Fix (https://github.com/slopkhayzo/DeadSpace3-RawInputMouseFix)
   The Raw Input Mouse Fix add-on (in the add-ons download, RawInputMouseFix\DS3RawMouse.asi; source in
   sdk/raw_mouse) is based on the fix by slopkhayzo, version 1.1.2. All credit for the fix is theirs. The add-on is
   that fix rebuilt with the mod SDK, plus one setting (UseGameSlider, on by default: the in-game mouse slider scales
   the fix's sensitivity); sdk/raw_mouse/CHANGELOG.txt lists every change. MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 slopkhayzo:
   LICENSE-RawInputMouseFix.txt in the add-on's folder and in sdk/raw_mouse.
